Lumen final pilot report, revision 2, 2026-08-10.
Twenty-four participants were randomized, 12 to intervention and 12 to control.
The six-week analysis included 10 intervention participants and 11 control participants.
Mean systolic blood-pressure change from baseline was -6 mmHg in intervention and -4 mmHg in control.
These are descriptive means for the analyzed participants. No standard errors, confidence intervals or significance tests are reported.
