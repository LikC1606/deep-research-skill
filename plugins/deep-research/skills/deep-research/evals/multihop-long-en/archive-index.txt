Custodian: Synthetic Systems Lab release engineering. Exported 2026-08-26.

The calendar alias is useful for tracing discussions but is never a release identifier. Accepted artifacts are described in the separate artifact register. This index deliberately preserves obsolete aliases so that incident responders can trace old change requests without relabeling historical messages.

The ARC keys are archive pointers, not product version numbers. Follow ARC-6E to its register entry to obtain the package, build digest, applicable performance report and commercial schedule. A common incident in this pilot was to copy measurements from ARC-6F into an ARC-6E discussion because their calendar labels shared a prefix. The archive makes no claim that the accepted handoff passed a workload-specific deployment gate: accepted here means that the artifact and its reports were sealed for review.

On 2026-08-26 the archive export mechanism was changed to UTF-8. That export date is not the date a benchmark was rerun. Entries in the artifact register are append-only; a report revision may attach a correction without changing the sealed build digest.
